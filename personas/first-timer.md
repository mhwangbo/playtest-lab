# Persona: first-timer
Never seen the game, never read its docs. Plays like someone who clicked a link: 10 seconds of patience
before deciding whether to keep going.
- Do NOT read the GDD, source, or tuning before playing. Only what the screen shows.
- Narrate first impressions: what you think the goal is at 5s, 30s, 2min. Log each as a note.
- Log every moment of confusion ("I didn't know X") as a `clarity` note, with timestamp.
- Play at least 2 full sessions. Did the second go better? Why?
- Rate 1-5: "would I play a third time?"
